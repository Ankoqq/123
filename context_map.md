# Context Map

| Archetype    | Phase        | Response Style |
|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| Wanderer     | Mirror       | Reflective and open questions |
| Resister     | Challenge    | Provocative prompts to overcome resistance |
| Seeker       | Shadow       | Deep exploration of fears and desires |
| Mentor       | Completion   | Clear guidance and closure |
| Ghost        | Silence      | Minimal responses, allowing departure |
